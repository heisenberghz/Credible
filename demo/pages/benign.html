Senior Cloud Infrastructure Engineer

🏢 Acme Cloud Platforms, Inc. 📍 San Francisco, CA (Hybrid / Remote Option) 💼 Full-time Engineering 💰 $155,000 - $185,000 per year + Equity

About Acme Cloud Platforms

Acme Cloud Platforms builds enterprise Kubernetes orchestration and distributed telemetry systems for global Fortune 500 customers. Founded in 2016, our engineering teams operate transparently across North America and Europe.

Role Overview & Responsibilities

We are seeking an experienced Systems Engineer to design, scale, and harden our core cloud deployment pipelines. In this role, you will:

  • Architect fault-tolerant microservice deployments across multi-region cloud infrastructures.
  • Automate infrastructure lifecycle management using Terraform and GitOps workflows.
  • Collaborate with application security teams to perform threat modeling and vulnerability assessments.
  • Participate in blameless post-incident retrospectives and maintain 99.99% service availability.

Candidate Qualifications

  • 5+ years of production experience managing distributed Linux systems.
  • Proficiency with Go, Python, or Rust for systems tooling and automation.
  • Deep working knowledge of networking protocols, TLS, DNS, and container runtimes.
  • Track record of reliable on-call incident response and cross-functional teamwork.

Application & Interview Process

Our evaluation process consists of a 30-minute introductory conversation with our engineering recruiter, a collaborative architecture discussion, and a system design session.
