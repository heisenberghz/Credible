About the Project

Nova Research Labs is an informal research initiative benchmarking open multimodal models. We crowd-source human annotations, rating model prompt responses for accuracy, coherence, and safety alignment.

What You Will Do

  • Review synthetic conversational datasets and score factual accuracy.
  • Submit standardized annotation feedback via our web dashboard.
  • Participate in periodic quality recalibration sessions.

Payment & Communication

Compensation is disbursed per validated batch. We never ask contributors to pay any fee or deposit money to join. However, contributor coordination is conducted primarily through open messaging groups rather than corporate email servers.

To apply, submit a portfolio or resume summarizing any prior annotation experience to our volunteer coordinator.
